Privacy Policy

Last updated February 2026

Your Data, Your Control

Signal is built on a fundamental principle: your email data belongs to you. We access only what is necessary to provide inbox sorting and automation, and we never sell, share, or monetize your personal information.

What We Access

  • Email metadata (sender, subject, timestamp) for sorting and rule evaluation
  • Email content is processed locally on your Mac — never transmitted to our servers
  • Gmail API access via OAuth 2.0 with minimal required scopes
  • Anonymous usage analytics to improve Signal (can be disabled in settings)

What We Never Do

  • Sell or share your email data with third parties
  • Store email content on our servers
  • Use your data for advertising or profiling
  • Access your inbox when Signal is not actively running

Data Storage

Signal stores your automation rules and preferences locally on your Mac using encrypted storage. Your Gmail OAuth token is stored securely in the macOS Keychain. No email content is ever persisted outside your device.

Beta Waitlist

If you submit your email address via the waitlist form, we store it solely for the purpose of sending you a beta invitation. You can request removal at any time by contacting us.
